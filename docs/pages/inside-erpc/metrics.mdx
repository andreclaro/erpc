# Metrics — reading erpc_* like a pro

The [metrics reference](/reference/metrics) lists every series eRPC exports. This page
teaches the shape underneath: **each metric family fires at exactly one decision point**
in the request path, so a dashboard is a map of commitments — a selection reason, a retry
reason, an attempt outcome — not a bag of counters.

The widget below replays one cold read with a hedge, lighting up the exact series each
stage emits as the playhead crosses it. Flip the toggle to a cache HIT and count what
goes *absent* — that absence is the single most misread property of this metrics surface.

<MetricAnatomy caption="A cold read touches 11 series across 6 families; the cancelled hedge-loser is accounted as an outcome, never as an error. Toggle to a cache HIT: 4 series, and the upstream families are absent — not zero." />

## The workflow

1. **Ingress emits nothing on a healthy pass.** The first counter fires one layer down,
   when `Project.Forward` commits to serving the call:
   `erpc_network_request_received_total` +1 (`erpc/projects.go:152`). Auth only emits on
   failure (`erpc_auth_failed_total`, `telemetry/metrics.go:941-945`).
2. **The cache GET misses** — `erpc_cache_get_success_miss_total` +1 with
   `reason="connector_miss"` (`architecture/evm/json_rpc_cache.go:541`). The `reason`
   label is what separates a cold cache from a *broken* one
   (`telemetry/metrics.go:848-858`).
3. **The failsafe executor arms a hedge**: `erpc_upstream_selection_total` +1 with
   `reason="hedge"`, and the adaptive delay is recorded in
   `erpc_network_hedge_delay_seconds` (`telemetry/metrics.go:1121-1126`).
4. **Two attempts race.** Both terminate in `erpc_upstream_attempt_outcome_total`
   (one `success`, one `cancelled`) — but the cancelled hedge-loser never reaches the
   error counters; the churn surfaces only in `erpc_network_hedge_discards_total` +1,
   while `erpc_network_hedge_winner_total` +1 marks the keeper
   (`upstream/upstream.go:730-751`, `erpc/network_executor.go:638`).
5. **The response commits**: `erpc_network_successful_request_total` +1 and
   `erpc_network_request_duration_seconds` observed (`erpc/projects.go:250`,
   `erpc/projects.go:269`) — the denominator behind every "how healthy is my traffic"
   ratio.
6. **Only after the 200 leaves**, the async cache writer emits
   `erpc_cache_set_success_total` +1 (`architecture/evm/json_rpc_cache.go:873`) — client
   latency never pays for bookkeeping.

## In the source

### The registry — `telemetry/metrics.go`

Every `erpc_*` name, label set, and help string lives in one file. The help strings are
part of the contract: they carry the reason-label enums, the deprecation notice on
`erpc_rate_limiter_budget_decision_total` (`telemetry/metrics.go:791-795`), and the
"transitions, not evaluations" warnings on the served-tip family
(`telemetry/metrics.go:157-198`). High-cardinality counters go through `CounterHandle`
so the health tracker's idle sweep can evict stale label combinations
(`telemetry/metrics.go` + `health/tracker.go`).

### Emission sites by scope

- **Network scope** — received/successful/failed/duration all fire in `Project.Forward`
  (`erpc/projects.go:152`, `:250`, `:269`, `:332`); hedge discards at
  `erpc/networks.go:2835`; retry reasons at `erpc/network_executor.go:314`.
- **Upstream scope** — one attempt-outcome and one selection increment per attempt in the
  deferred record (`upstream/upstream.go:690-707`); `erpc_upstream_request_total` at
  `:757`; hedge exclusion from the health-scoring counters at `:745-751` and `:859-866`.
- **Cache scope** — hit/miss in `architecture/evm/json_rpc_cache.go:598` and `:541`; the
  async SET at `:873`; cache-connector executor attempts at `data/cache_executor.go:119`.
- **Consensus scope** — outcomes at `consensus/executor.go:1601`; the analyzer goroutine
  keeps emitting responses-collected even after the caller abandons the request
  (`consensus/executor.go:320-337`).
- **Error-label hygiene** — the `error` label is `ErrorFingerprint`: sanitized and capped
  at 256 characters, never a raw error string (`common/errors.go:117-125`).

## First principles

> **Counters are denominators.** Every ratio worth alerting on divides by
> `erpc_upstream_attempt_outcome_total` or `erpc_upstream_request_total` — per network,
> upstream, and method. A bare counter tells you volume; only a rate tells you health.

> **Metrics fire at decision points, not wrappers.** Each family maps to one place where
> eRPC commits to an outcome: a selection reason, a retry reason, an attempt outcome, a
> consensus verdict. If you know the pipeline, you can predict the metric — and vice versa.

> **Absent is not zero.** Families that never ran emit nothing — a cache HIT produces no
> upstream series at all — and some series are deliberately absent by mode
> (`erpc_network_served_tip_lag_blocks` in default MAX mode). Build queries that survive
> absence.

### How it works

All series live in a single registry (`telemetry/metrics.go`), built with `promauto` at
package init. Filter-aware histograms get their buckets from config
(`SetHistogramBuckets`, `telemetry/metrics.go:1250-1254`); counter/histogram handles that
carry caller-controlled labels (method, user, agent name) go through
`CounterHandle`/`ObserverHandle` so the health tracker can sweep and delete stale series
every few minutes. The `error` label is always `common.ErrorFingerprint` — non-alphanumerics
collapsed, 256-char cap (`common/errors.go:117-125`) — so error cardinality stays bounded.

### Commonly misread — the ten that bite

1. **`erpc_upstream_block_head_large_rollback` is a sticky gauge.** It is set to the size
   of the last rollback and never reset: `> 0` latches forever and `rate()`/`increase()`
   are meaningless (`telemetry/metrics.go:568-577`). Alert on transition-to-nonzero via an
   `changes()`-style rule or scrape-time reset handling, not on the raw value.
2. **Served-tip regression/trajectory count transitions, not evaluations.** The pick runs
   several times per request; these counters deliberately count state entries, so any
   sustained non-zero rate is alert-worthy (`telemetry/metrics.go:157-198`).
3. **`erpc_network_served_tip_lag_blocks` is ABSENT, not 0**, when served-tip is in
   default MAX mode (`telemetry/metrics.go:133-142`). Use `or on(...)` vector matching in
   queries that must treat it as zero.
4. **`erpc_rate_limiter_budget_decision_total` is deprecated** in favor of
   `erpc_rate_limits_total` (`telemetry/metrics.go:791-795`). Old dashboards keep working
   but new rules should use the unified counter.
5. **The `finality` label is dual-meaning.** On the rollback gauge it is the head axis
   (`latest`/`finalized`); on the request/attempt families it is a
   `common.DataFinalityState` (`telemetry/metrics.go:562-567`). Never join the two
   without a label rewrite.
6. **`cache_executor_attempt_total{outcome="success"}` ≠ cache hit.** It means "answered
   within budget" — a gRPC connector miss is a null-result success. Hit/miss live in
   `erpc_cache_get_success_hit/miss_total` (`telemetry/metrics.go:742-757`).
7. **`erpc_upstream_request_total` (pre-dispatch) vs `erpc_upstream_misbehavior_total`
   (post-resolution) label the same request differently** — the former often reports
   `finality="unknown"` while the latter reports the resolved finality, so per-finality
   misbehavior ratios can exceed 100%. Divide on unfiltered totals
   (`telemetry/metrics.go:595-609`).
8. **Hedge attempts are excluded from the health-scoring rate counters**
   (`upstream/upstream.go:745-751`, `:842-866`) — a slow-but-functional upstream does not
   get double-penalized for losing hedge races. The tell for heavy hedge churn is
   `erpc_network_hedge_discards_total` (`telemetry/metrics.go:657-661`), not error rates.
9. **Catch-up retries have a normal non-zero baseline on live chains.**
   `missing_data` / `block_unavailable` / `empty_result` retries wait ~one block by
   design — judge the pressure trend and per-chain wait sanity, never raw counts
   (`monitoring/catch-up-metrics.md`, `telemetry/metrics.go:1135-1150`).
10. **`erpc_consensus_responses_collected` is a histogram of response counts** (buckets
    1–10), not a duration — `histogram_quantile` on it answers "how many upstreams
    replied before the decision", nothing about time (`telemetry/metrics.go:1152-1157`).
